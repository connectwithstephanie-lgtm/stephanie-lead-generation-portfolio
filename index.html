My process

A clear six-step system, with client decisions built into the workflow so targeting and messaging stay aligned with your business.

01 — DEFINEUnderstand your ideal client.

We clarify who you want to target, industries, decision-maker roles, locations, company characteristics, your offer, and value proposition.

02 — BUILDFind the right prospects.

I research and build a targeted LinkedIn prospect list with relevant prospects, decision-maker information, profiles, company details, lead status, and organized data.

03 — CONNECTStart relevant conversations.

I help manage LinkedIn outreach using messaging aligned with your offer and target audience. The focus is reaching the right people with the right context.

04 — ENGAGEFollow up without being pushy.

I track connections, replies, interested prospects, follow-ups, positive conversations, and unqualified leads so conversations don't get lost.

05 — QUALIFYSeparate interest from opportunity.

I help identify prospects who show relevant interest and organize them according to agreed qualification criteria.

06 — HAND OFFTurn qualified leads into sales conversations.

Qualified opportunities are organized and handed over to you or your sales team for the next step.
