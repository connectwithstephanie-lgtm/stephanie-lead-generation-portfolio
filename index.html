How I build your pipeline

From Ideal Client Profile to Qualified Prospect List — a structured process designed to produce actionable B2B leads, not just a database of contacts.

01 Define the ICPUnderstand exactly who you want to reach.

Target industry, company size, location, job titles, decision-makers, business requirements and exclusion criteria.

02 Find Target CompaniesResearch companies that match your criteria.

Sources may include LinkedIn, Google, company websites, Apollo, Companies House, industry directories, and property or business databases.

03 Identify Decision-MakersFind the people who influence purchasing decisions.

Typical contacts include founders, CEOs, managing directors, directors, heads of department, investors and business owners.

04 Research & EnrichTurn company research into actionable lead data.

Company, website, decision-maker, job title, LinkedIn, business email, phone, location, industry, company size and relevant notes.

05 Qualify & VerifyCheck every prospect against the client's criteria.

I review ICP fit, company, decision-maker, contact information, company activity, location and other client-specific requirements.

06 Organize the CRMMove qualified leads into a clean, usable database.

Example workflow: Research → Google Sheets → CRM. Leads can be organized by status, industry, location, decision-maker, priority, outreach status and response.

07 Outreach & Follow-UpSupport the next stage of the sales process.

Email outreach, LinkedIn outreach, personalized messaging, follow-up sequences and response tracking.

08 AutomationConnect the workflow where automation makes sense.

Using Make.com, Zapier, Google Sheets, Gmail, Apollo and ClickUp to reduce repetitive work and keep lead information organized.

Example automated workflow
Google Sheets→New / Qualified Lead→CRM Update→Email Outreach→Response Detection→Follow-Up / Stop→CRM Update
